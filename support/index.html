Support

Privio is built by a solo indie developer. There is no support team, no ticket queue, no chatbot — just one human reading every email.

Typical reply window is under 24 hours, Istanbul time. Bug reports get the fastest turnaround — include your iOS or Android version and which tool you were using.

What to include in a support email

  • What you were trying to do — which tool, which step.
  • What you expected to happen versus what actually happened.
  • OS version + device model (e.g. "iOS 26.4 on iPhone 15 Pro Max" or "Android 16 on Pixel 9"). The in-app Contact Support button fills in the app version, build number and platform (iOS or Android) for me; nothing is sent until you send the email.
  • A screenshot if the issue is visual or there is an error message.

Do not attach any PDF that contains sensitive information when filing a bug report. Privio runs on-device specifically so your documents stay private; please keep them off support email too.

Common questions

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

iPhone or iPad: Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Privio → Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Privio → Cancel. You keep access until the current period ends. No questions, no retention dark patterns from our side.

Where are my scanned and edited PDFs saved?

When you save, share or export a result, it goes to Privio's own folder: on iPhone and iPad, that is the Files app → On My iPhone → Privio (or On My iPad); on Android, the app's private storage. Results you have not saved stay in a hidden staging folder and are deleted at the next app start once they are more than a day old. The folder is not in iCloud Drive, but like other app data it is included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup; Android's device backup) if you back up your device. Both iOS and Android encrypt app storage when the device has a passcode or screen lock. Details: Privacy Policy, section 4.

Does Privio upload my PDFs anywhere?

No. Your documents are processed on your device, and the app has no feature that uploads document content. The app does make a few network connections of its own: App Store or Google Play purchase checks when it starts, optional usage statistics through Firebase Analytics (on by default; turn them off in Settings → Privacy & data), Firebase technical diagnostics, and on Android, Google ML Kit library diagnostics. None of them contains your documents or file names. There are no ads, advertising identifiers or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. The Privacy Policy lists them.

The scanner is asking for camera access. Why?

On iPhone and iPad, Privio uses Apple's VisionKit document scanner, which needs the camera at the moment you tap "Scan to PDF" to capture pages. The pages are processed on the device and the finished PDF is saved in Privio's storage; nothing is uploaded. If you decline the permission, the scanner is disabled but every other tool still works — you can import PDFs and images through the Files app or the share sheet instead. On Android, the scanner is Google's ML Kit document scanner, which runs inside Google Play services, so Privio itself does not ask for camera access.

I redacted a PDF. Is the text really gone?

For the matches Privio finds and boxes correctly, yes. Redact renders every page of the document to an image, burns black boxes over the matched words, and builds a new PDF from those images, so the original text layer, metadata, attachments, form fields, links and bookmarks are not carried over. If "Keep non-redacted text searchable" (OCR-back) is on — it is by default — Privio adds a new, invisible text layer recognised from the redacted images, so it contains only what is still visible on the page. Privio can only redact what it finds: matching works on the page's text layer, so text inside images and values typed into form fields are not found, and in this version the boxes land in the wrong place on rotated pages. Read How Privio redacts for the known limitations, and before sharing a redacted PDF, search it for each term and look at every page.

Receipt scanner extracted the wrong total / date / vendor.

Receipt parsing uses on-device OCR plus regex heuristics — fast and private, but it can miss things on unusual formats. Every field is editable before you tap Save, and the original PDF stays untouched. If a specific receipt format reliably fails for you, email a redacted screenshot and I will tune the parser for that format in the next update.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new phone?

Privio checks your purchases with the store each time it starts, so Pro usually comes back by itself. If it does not, open Privio → Settings → Account → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone, iPad) or Google account (Android) you used to buy Pro. If it still does not come back, email me and I will help — usually the issue is a different account by mistake.

I want a feature that is not in the app.

Send me the use case (one paragraph is plenty) and what you currently do without it. I read every request and tend to prioritise the ones that match the lawyer / accountant / pro wedge — Privio is intentionally not trying to be every PDF app for every user.

Ratings

If Privio is genuinely helping you, leaving a quick rating on the App Store or Google Play is the single most valuable thing you can do for a solo indie developer — it is how new users discover the app. If you have Pro, the store's rating prompt may appear after a few successful runs, at most once every 90 days; you can also leave a review any time from the store listing.
