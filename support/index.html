Support

Privio is built by a solo indie developer. There is no support team, no ticket queue, no chatbot — just one human reading every email.

Typical reply window is under 24 hours, Istanbul time. Bug reports get the fastest turnaround — include your iOS or Android version and which tool you were using.

What to include in a support email

  • What you were trying to do — which tool, which step.
  • What you expected to happen versus what actually happened.
  • OS version + device model (e.g. "iOS 26.4 on iPhone 15 Pro Max" or "Android 16 on Pixel 9"). The in-app Contact Support button fills in the app version, build number and platform (iOS or Android) for me; nothing is sent until you send the email.
  • A screenshot if the issue is visual or there is an error message.

Do not attach any PDF that contains sensitive information when filing a bug report. Privio processes your documents on the device so that it never has to upload them; please keep them off support email too.

Common questions

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Every tool opens and runs free on your own document, with no account and no trial. Without Pro you can save or share the PDFs Privio makes, with a PDF Privio watermark on every page: a large diagonal "PDF Privio" and a footer line. Pro removes the watermark, lets you copy or export text results (Extract text, Compare PDFs, Find sensitive data and Summarize PDF) and opens batch processing and the receipt scanner. While you are still on the result screen, Remove watermark lets you buy Pro, and the clean file then replaces the watermarked copy in Privio's folder. Watermarked files from earlier results, and copies you have already shared or saved outside Privio, keep the watermark. There are no ads, free or Pro. In version 1.1.5, saving, sharing or exporting any result requires Pro.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

iPhone or iPad: Settings → tap your name at the top → Subscriptions → Privio → Cancel Subscription. Android: Google Play → tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Privio → Cancel. You keep access until the current period ends. No questions, no retention dark patterns from our side.

Where are my scanned and edited PDFs saved?

When you save, share or export a result, it goes to Privio's own folder: on iPhone and iPad, that is the Files app → On My iPhone → Privio (or On My iPad); on Android, the app's private storage. Without Pro, the copy with the watermark goes there, and the result without it stays in a hidden staging folder with any results you have not saved; those are deleted at the next app start once they are more than a day old. If you buy Pro on the same result screen, the clean file replaces the watermarked copy. The folder is not in iCloud Drive, but like other app data it is included in your iPhone or iPad backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), and Android's device backup can include it, if you back up your device; to keep it out of iCloud Backup, see the next question. Both iOS and Android encrypt app storage when the device has a passcode or screen lock. Details: Privacy Policy, section 4.

Can I keep Privio's files out of iCloud Backup?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad. Privio never uploads your documents, but your device's backups include its files like other app data. Three separate settings decide what happens to them:

  • Leave Privio out of iCloud Backup. Open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud → Storage (or Manage Account Storage) → Backups, tap this device, turn off Privio and tap Turn Off. iOS stops backing up Privio's files to iCloud and deletes the copies already in iCloud Backup. The files stay on your device, but a device restored from iCloud Backup will not have them. Apple: Manage your iCloud storage.
  • Computer backups. That switch does not affect a backup to a Mac or PC, which still includes Privio's files. To encrypt it, tick Encrypt local backup in Finder on a Mac, or in the Apple Devices app or iTunes on a PC.
  • Advanced Data Protection. If you keep Privio in iCloud Backup, turning on Advanced Data Protection (Settings → your name → iCloud → Advanced Data Protection) makes iCloud Backup end-to-end encrypted, so Apple no longer holds its keys. The backup is still uploaded to iCloud: Advanced Data Protection changes who can read it, not where it goes. It needs two-factor authentication for your Apple Account, a device passcode, and a recovery contact or recovery key. Apple: How to turn on Advanced Data Protection for iCloud.

On Android, the device backup can include Privio's files. Details: Privacy Policy, section 4.5.

Does Privio upload my PDFs anywhere?

No. Your documents are processed on your device, and the app has no feature that uploads document content. The app does make a few network connections of its own: App Store or Google Play purchase checks when it starts, optional usage statistics through Firebase Analytics (on by default for the free version, off by default with Pro; change it in Settings → Privacy & data), Firebase technical diagnostics, and on Android, Google ML Kit library diagnostics. None of them contains your documents or file names. There are no ads, advertising identifiers or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. The Privacy Policy lists them.

How does Password protect encrypt a PDF?

With AES-256, in one of two ways, depending on the version of Privio that set the password:

  • Version 1.1.7 and later. A password you set uses the PDF 2.0 method (revision 6) and must be 8 to 127 printable ASCII characters: A–Z, a–z, 0–9, spaces and ASCII symbols. Apple's PDF viewers (Files, Mail, Safari, Preview) handle accented letters and other alphabets in a password differently from other PDF apps, so such a password might open the file in one and not the other.
  • Version 1.1.6 and earlier. Privio uses revision 5, the older AES-256 method introduced with Acrobat 9, which PDF 2.0 replaced with revision 6, and accepts passwords of 4 or more characters.

Updating Privio does not change files you have already protected: they keep revision 5. Version 1.1.7 still removes their passwords; to move such a file to revision 6, remove its password and protect it again. Filing with a court? Federal CM/ECF and many courts' e-filing systems reject password-protected or encrypted PDFs, so upload an unprotected copy.

The scanner is asking for camera access. Why?

On iPhone and iPad, Privio uses Apple's VisionKit document scanner, which needs the camera at the moment you tap "Scan to PDF" to capture pages. The pages are processed on the device and the finished PDF is saved in Privio's storage; nothing is uploaded. If you decline the permission, the scanner is disabled but every other tool still works — you can import PDFs and images through the Files app or the share sheet instead. On Android, the scanner is Google's ML Kit document scanner, which runs inside Google Play services, so Privio itself does not ask for camera access.

I redacted a PDF. Is the text really gone?

For the matches Privio finds and boxes correctly, yes. Redact renders every page of the document to an image, burns black boxes over the matched words, and builds a new PDF from those images, so the original text layer, metadata, attachments, form fields, links and bookmarks are not carried over. If "Keep non-redacted text searchable" (OCR-back) is on — it is by default — Privio adds a new, invisible text layer recognised from the redacted images, so it contains only what is still visible on the page. Privio can only redact what it finds: matching works on the page's text layer, so text inside images and values typed into form fields are not found (on iOS, form-field values are also left out of the redacted copy). Pages it could not check are listed on the result screen, and Privio asks you to confirm before you export them. Read How Privio redacts for the known limitations, and before sharing a redacted PDF, search it for each term and look at every page.

Can I fill USCIS forms, such as the I-485?

Only partly. For USCIS forms, use Adobe Acrobat Reader. We checked the I-485 (edition 09/18/26): it is an XFA form, built on a scripted form layer that Adobe Reader runs. Privio does not run form scripts; it fills the standard (AcroForm) form fields that the same file also contains. In that layer:

  • 131 of the form’s 730 fields are marked read-only, including every A-Number box. Privio honours the read-only flag, so in Privio the A-Number cannot be typed anywhere. In Adobe Reader you type it once, in Part 1, Item 4 on page 2, and the other A-Number boxes show the same value.
  • Many of the other read-only fields are conditional fields that the form’s scripts open in Adobe Reader.
  • When Privio saves the form, flattened or not, the 2D barcode is drawn as plain text instead, for example “I-485|09/18/26|1”. The barcode holds only the form name, edition and page number, not your answers.

Privio fills standard PDF forms: text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons and choice lists.

Receipt scanner extracted the wrong total / date / vendor.

Receipt parsing uses on-device OCR plus regex heuristics — fast and private, but it can miss things on unusual formats. Every field is editable before you tap Save, and the original PDF stays untouched. If a specific receipt format reliably fails for you, email a redacted screenshot and I will tune the parser for that format in the next update.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new phone?

Privio checks your purchases with the store each time it starts, so Pro usually comes back by itself. If it does not, open Privio → Settings → Account → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone, iPad) or Google account (Android) you used to buy Pro. If it still does not come back, email me and I will help — usually the issue is a different account by mistake.

I want a feature that is not in the app.

Send me the use case (one paragraph is plenty) and what you currently do without it. I read every request and tend to prioritise the ones that match the lawyer / accountant / pro wedge — Privio is intentionally not trying to be every PDF app for every user.

Ratings
