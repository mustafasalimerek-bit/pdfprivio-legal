PDF Privio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-XX
Applies to: PDF Privio 1.1.5 and later, on iOS and Android; section 10 covers versions 1.1.1 to 1.1.3
Operator: Erek Studio (Mustafa Salim Erek, sole proprietor)
Contact: mustafasalimerek@gmail.com
App: PDF Privio (shown on the home screen as “Privio”), com.erekstudio.pdfprivio

Summary

  • Your documents are processed on your device. There is no Erek Studio server and no account. The app has no feature that uploads document content: your PDFs, page images, recognized text, signatures, form entries, redaction search terms and receipt details are not sent to Erek Studio, to Firebase or to any other service, unless you share or export them yourself or use an Apple feature such as Visual Look Up (section 1.2).
  • The app does make some network connections of its own. Section 1 lists them: App Store or Google Play purchase checks each time the app starts, optional usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase (on by default for the free version, off by default with Pro; you can change it in Settings), Firebase technical diagnostics, and on Android, Google’s ML Kit diagnostics. None of them contains document content or file names.
  • No ads and no tracking. There is no advertising SDK, no access to your advertising identifier and no third-party crash-reporting SDK. On iOS, Privio asks Apple’s App Tracking Transparency question; once you answer, iOS does not show it again, and nothing in the app depends on your answer (section 2 explains why it is asked).
  • Privio keeps an audit log on your device — one entry per tool run, with file names, sizes, a partial file hash and the tool’s settings (section 4.3). You can turn it off, or keep file names and labels out of it. It does not leave the device unless you export it.

1. Network connections

This list is drawn from the app’s source code and from the documentation of the software development kits (SDKs) it includes. It is not the result of an independent network audit.

1.1 Connections made without a tap from you

  • App Store or Google Play purchase checks. Each time the app starts, it asks the store whether purchases are available, loads the prices of the three Pro products and quietly restores your purchases, so Pro stays active after a reinstall or on a new device. Buying or restoring Pro also goes through the store. These requests are made by Apple’s StoreKit or Google Play Billing and contain no document content. Erek Studio runs no receipt-validation server and never receives your payment card, billing address, Apple ID or Google account.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). On by default for the free version and off by default with Pro; change it in Settings → Privacy & data → Share usage statistics. What they contain is listed in section 2.
  • Firebase technical diagnostics. The Firebase SDK can contact Google to register and maintain the app installation (Firebase Installations). This involves an installation identifier and technical SDK and device information, and it can happen even when usage statistics are off. The in-app switch is not a promise that all Google SDK traffic stops.
  • Google ML Kit diagnostics (Android only). On Android, OCR and the document scanner use Google ML Kit, which processes your pages on the device. ML Kit separately sends Google diagnostic and usage data about the library: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), the app’s package name and version, an installation or device identifier, performance metrics such as latency, API configuration such as image format and resolution, input and output sizes, and event and error codes. Google’s disclosure lists metadata of this kind; it does not list page images or recognized text. Erek Studio does not receive these reports, and the usage-statistics switch does not turn them off. Google’s disclosure: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.
  • Document scanner module (Android only). The Android scanner is supplied by Google Play services, which may download the scanner module the first time you use it.
  • Store rating prompt. If you have Pro, after at least three successful tool runs and two days after installation, the app may ask the App Store or Google Play to show the system rating prompt — at most once every 90 days. The store decides whether anything is shown.

1.2 Connections made only when you tap

  • Share, Open in, Save and Export. The system share sheet or file picker hands the file to the app or place you choose (Mail, Files, AirDrop, Google Drive and so on). That destination’s privacy policy then applies.
  • Links. Privacy Policy, Terms, Manage subscription, the store page and Redeem code open in your browser or store app. Contact Support opens your mail app with a draft that includes the app version, build number and platform (iOS or Android); nothing is sent until you send the email.
  • Apple features in the Live Text view (iOS only). The Live Text view uses Apple’s Quick Look viewer. Apple features inside it, such as Visual Look Up, are operated by Apple and may send Apple limited information when you use them, under Apple’s privacy policy.
  • Files stored in the cloud. If you pick a file kept in iCloud Drive, Google Drive or another provider, that provider may download it to your device.

1.3 What happens on the device

Scanning, OCR, redaction, signing, form filling, comparing, the sensitive-data scan, receipt extraction and every other tool process your documents on the device. Summaries use Apple’s on-device model through the Foundation Models framework (iOS 26 or later; not available on Android). The app does not download fonts.

2. Usage statistics

Usage statistics are on by default for the free version and off by default with Pro. If you have not changed the setting yourself, it follows your plan: after you buy Pro, or tap Restore purchases, statistics stay on until the app is next started and are off from then on; a Pro user who reinstalls Privio or installs it on a new device starts with them off. A choice you make in Settings → Privacy & data → Share usage statistics always wins over the default. Turning them off does not change tool access, prices, purchases or document processing. It stops future Analytics collection; it does not delete statistics Google has already received.

When they are on, the app sends Google Analytics for Firebase these events and no others: onboarding completed; tool opened, with the tool’s name from a fixed list; paywall shown, with where it was opened from (and the tool, if any); paywall closed, with whether a purchase was made; purchase started and its outcome, with the plan (monthly, yearly or lifetime), the result (success, cancelled, pending or failed) and a fixed error category; restore outcome, with the type of purchase found; scan completed, with a page-count range (1, 2–5, 6–20 or 21+); export tapped, with the tool; and export completed, with the tool and the output type (PDF, image, text or other). The only user property the app sets is whether Pro is active. The app never sets a user ID.

Firebase also records its own automatic events, such as first open, session start and in-app purchases (which can include the product ID, product name and price), together with an app-instance identifier, app, device and OS information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. On Android, Firebase can also read the Google Play install referrer — the store campaign or source that led to the installation, if any. These statistics are pseudonymous, not anonymous: the app-instance identifier links events from the same installation. They never include your name, email address, document names, file paths, document text, receipt contents, signatures or images.

Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad-personalization consent are set to denied. Collection of the iOS IDFA and IDFV and the Android Advertising ID and SSAID, automatic screen reporting and iOS ad-network registration are disabled.

On iOS, Privio asks the App Tracking Transparency question right after onboarding, at launch if you finished onboarding earlier, and again when you return to the app if the question was closed without an answer. iOS shows it only until you answer it. Privio does not track you or read your advertising identifier, and nothing in the app depends on your answer. The question is there because Privio’s App Store privacy label still lists tracking from the earlier ad-supported version, and App Review turned down an update that did not ask it. Android has no such question.

Erek Studio uses the reports only to see how the app is used and where people stop before finishing a task or a purchase. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. Google processes it under its own terms: firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Permissions

  • Camera (iOS). Requested the first time you scan. Apple’s VisionKit document camera captures the pages and the finished PDF is written to the app’s storage. On Android, the scanner runs inside Google Play services and Privio does not hold the camera permission itself.
  • Photos and files. The system photo and document pickers give the app only the items you select.

The app does not request location, contacts, microphone or calendar permissions. On iOS it asks the App Tracking Transparency question (see section 2); once you answer, iOS does not show it again, and the answer is not used. The approximate location in usage statistics comes from your IP address, not from GPS or a location permission.

4. What is stored on your device

4.1 Your files

  • Saved results. When you save, share or export a result (Pro), it is written to the app’s Documents folder. On iOS that folder is visible in the Files app under On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Privio. On Android it is the app’s private storage.
  • Results you have not saved. A tool’s result first goes to a private staging folder that the Files app does not show. If you never save, share or export it, it is deleted the next time the app starts, once it is more than 24 hours old.
  • Files shared into Privio (iOS). A file you send to Privio from another app through the share sheet is copied into the Imported folder inside Documents and stays there until you delete it.

4.2 Temporary files

While a tool runs, Privio writes page images to the app’s private temporary folder — for example, every page rendered as a JPEG for OCR or redaction. The system file picker also puts a copy of each file you pick into the app’s private temporary or cache folder.

  • Page images made for redaction and for text recognition are deleted when the operation ends — whether it finishes, fails or is cancelled.
  • Anything an operation could not clean up — after a crash or force-quit, a cancelled page render, the image of a PDF receipt’s first page, the PDF renderer’s page images, and the copies the system file picker makes of the files you pick — is deleted the next time the app starts, before any new work begins. On Android, copies of files shared into Privio from other apps are deleted at launch once they are more than 15 minutes old.

The temporary folder is inside the app’s sandbox, is not shown in the Files app and is not included in device backups. One exception on iOS: the PDF renderer writes each page image briefly to a pdf_renderer_cache folder inside the Documents folder and deletes it as soon as the image has been read; if the app stops at that moment, the image stays there — where the Files app shows it — until the next launch deletes it.

4.3 Audit log

Privio writes an audit-log entry automatically each time most tools finish a run. The log stays on your device; you can view it, export it as a CSV file or clear it in Settings → Privacy & data → Audit log. The same screen has two switches, both on by default:

  • Keep an audit log. Turning it off stops new entries. Existing entries stay until you clear them.
  • Include file names and labels. Turning it off keeps file names and free-text labels — the Bates prefix, signer name, watermark text and the names of merged files — out of new entries. Those entries keep only the tool, the time, file sizes, the partial file hash, and counts and settings that cannot identify a person or a document.

With both switches on, each entry records:

  • the date and time (UTC), the tool, and whether the run succeeded;
  • the input file’s name and size, and the first 16 hexadecimal characters of a SHA-256 hash of the input file’s first 1 MB;
  • the output file’s name and size;
  • the tool’s settings and counts — for example the Bates prefix and start number, the signer name, the names of up to ten merged files, the watermark text, page ranges, the number of redaction search terms, matches and pages that could not be checked, the number of sensitive-data findings per category, the currency and category of a saved receipt, and page, character or field counts.

The log never records redaction search terms, passwords (or their length) or the text of your documents. Password lengths recorded by earlier versions are removed from stored entries when the app starts. Entries are kept for 90 days, up to a maximum of 5,000 entries; older entries are removed when the app starts. The Compare and Bookmarks tools do not write entries.

The audit log and the receipt ledger are stored as database files in the app’s Documents folder. On iOS that folder is visible in the Files app, so these files appear there too and can be copied like any other file.

4.4 Other data on the device

  • Preferences such as your display name (used only for the greeting on the home screen), language and the usage-statistics switch, in the platform’s local key-value store.
  • Recent files: names and locations of recent files, for the Recent tab.
  • Home-screen widget: if you add Privio’s widget, it shows the names of your three most recent files, with the tool and time. You can hide the names in Settings → Personalization → Widget privacy.
  • Expense ledger: the receipt fields you save (date, vendor, total, currency, category and note).
  • Purchase state: a cached “Pro active” flag and the plan type, so the app knows your status before the store answers.

4.5 Backups and device security

The app’s Documents folder and its other app data are included in your device backups — iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iOS, Android’s device backup on Android — if you back up your device. Privio does not opt out of these backups. Both iOS and Android encrypt app storage on a device protected by a passcode or screen lock; set one.

5. Platform diagnostics

Apple and Google may separately provide crash, ANR (app not responding) or performance reports under their own operating-system and store settings — on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers; on Android, Google’s usage and diagnostics setting. PDF Privio does not include Crashlytics, Firebase Performance or any other third-party crash-reporting SDK. These platform settings are separate from Privio’s usage-statistics switch.

6. Retention, deletion and your rights

Erek Studio holds no copy of your documents, your audit log or your other on-device data. Deleting files in Privio, clearing the audit log or uninstalling the app removes the local copies; files you shared to another app, and copies in your backups, must be managed there.

Usage statistics are kept for the period set in the Google Analytics property’s data-retention setting. For information, access, correction, objection or deletion requests, contact mustafasalimerek@gmail.com. Because we do not collect an account identity, we may be unable to locate particular pseudonymous event records without more information. Do not send us documents or sensitive content with a request. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Store transaction records are kept and deleted by Apple or Google under their own policies.

7. Sale and sharing of data

Erek Studio does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as the California CCPA/CPRA and comparable US state laws use those terms. The app shows no ads and accesses no advertising identifier, except in versions 1.1.1 to 1.1.3 (see section 10).

8. International processing and security

Google may process usage statistics, Firebase diagnostics and ML Kit diagnostics outside your country under its data-processing terms. Your documents are processed on the device; Erek Studio does not maintain a cloud document database. Network traffic from the app uses encrypted connections, but no system can promise absolute security.

9. Children

PDF Privio is a document utility for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It is not enrolled in Google Play’s Designed for Families programme. Contact us if you believe information about a child requires attention.

10. Earlier versions (1.1.1 to 1.1.3)

Versions 1.1.1, 1.1.2 and 1.1.3 differ from this policy as follows:

  • Ads. Their free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, with Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent. To show and measure ads, Google collects advertising and device identifiers (on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; on Android, the Advertising ID), an approximate location derived from your IP address, ad-interaction data and diagnostic data about the ad SDK. The ad SDK never receives your documents, scanned images, OCR text or file names, and it is not started for Pro users. Google acts as an independent controller for this data: how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Under the California CCPA/CPRA and comparable US state laws, personalised ads can count as “sharing” personal information; you can opt out by declining tracking on iOS, opting out of ad personalisation in your Android settings, or using the consent form where it appears.
  • No Firebase. They include no Firebase, so section 2 and the Firebase items in section 1 do not apply to them.
  • Audit log. It has no switches, so every entry includes file names and labels, and it also records the length of a password you set.
  • Temporary files. Files left behind by an interrupted operation, and on iOS the PDF renderer’s page images, are not deleted at launch; they stay until the operating system clears them.
  • Rating prompt. The store rating prompt can also be shown to users without Pro.

Otherwise this policy describes those versions as well. Updating to the current version removes the advertising SDK.
