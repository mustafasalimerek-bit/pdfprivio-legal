This policy describes what data Privio does and does not collect. The short version is at the top.

TL;DR

Your documents never leave your device. Every PDF you scan, sign, redact, OCR, or convert is processed on your phone — whether you're on iPhone or Android. Erek Studio operates no servers, has no account system, and never receives your files, your OCR text, or your file names.

The free version of Privio shows ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects advertising and device identifiers, approximate (IP-derived) location, and app- and ad-interaction data in order to show and measure ads. The ad system never receives your documents. Privio Pro removes ads entirely — when you are Pro, the ad SDK is not started at all and makes no requests.

Apart from ads, the only outbound activity is what you explicitly tap: the system share sheet (the iOS Share Sheet or the Android share menu) to send a finished PDF to another app, or an in-app purchase through Apple's App Store or Google Play.

1. What Privio does not collect

This is the centerpiece of the product. Privio does not collect, transmit, or have any access to:

  • The contents of your PDFs — text, images, signatures, tables, form fields, anything.
  • OCR text extracted from your scans.
  • PII Scan results — SSNs, IBANs, account numbers, credit card numbers, or any other sensitive pattern Privio detects locally in your documents.
  • File names of the documents you open.
  • Your contacts, calendar, location, microphone, or any other system data outside of the explicit per-feature permissions described in section 3.
  • Product analytics. Privio does not include any product-analytics framework (Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.). We do not know how often you open the app, which tools you use, or how long your sessions are. The advertising SDK described in section 2.3 measures ads, not your use of the tools.
  • Crash reports sent to us. Privio does not include a crash-reporting framework (Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, Bugsnag, etc.). See sections 2.2 and 2.3 for the diagnostic data the platform and the ad SDK handle.
  • Account data. Privio has no account system. You don't sign up, log in, or hand us any identifier.

There are no servers operated by Erek Studio that receive your data, because there are no servers in this product. The advertising described in section 2.3 talks to Google's servers, never to ours, and never carries your documents.

2. What Privio actually does collect

Strictly limited to what is unavoidable for the app to function on Apple's platform:

2.1 In-app purchases (Apple App Store / Google Play)

If you upgrade to Privio Pro, the transaction is processed entirely by the platform's store — Apple's App Store on iOS, or Google Play on Android. The store shares a purchase/receipt token with the app so Privio can unlock paid features locally on your device. We never see your payment method, credit card number, billing address, Apple ID, or Google account.

The data each store itself collects during a purchase is governed by that store's own privacy policy: Apple — apple.com/legal/privacy; Google — policies.google.com/privacy.

2.2 Optional platform-level diagnostic sharing (controlled by you, not us)

Both Apple and Google offer an operating-system toggle that, if you turn it on, lets the platform forward anonymous crash and performance reports for any app — including Privio — to a dashboard the developer can read in aggregate. Privio itself contains no crash or product-analytics SDK; this happens at the OS/store level, never inside the app.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share with App Developers. Off by default. When on, Apple may share aggregate crash reports via App Store Connect.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Usage & diagnostics (plus the Play Store's equivalent). When on, Google may share aggregate crash/ANR and performance data (Android vitals) via the Play Console.
  • We do not control whether you have these on; the platform does.
  • The reports never include your file contents, file names, or any identifier we could link back to you personally.
  • You can turn either off at any time in your device settings without affecting how Privio works.

2.3 Advertising (free version only)

The free version of Privio is supported by ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, together with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. To show and measure an ad, Google collects and processes:

  • Advertising and device identifiers — the IDFA on iOS (only if you allow tracking, see below) and the Advertising ID on Android.
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address. Privio never requests your precise location and has no location permission.
  • App and ad interaction data — that an ad was requested, shown, clicked, or completed.
  • Diagnostic data about the ad SDK's own operation.

What the ad system never receives: your PDFs, the images you scan, OCR text, PII Scan results, file names, or anything else you create in Privio. The advertising SDK and the document pipeline share no data. This is the claim at the centre of the product, and adding ads did not change it.

Your controls:

  • iOS: the App Tracking Transparency dialog asks for your permission before the IDFA can be used. If you decline — or never answer — Privio still works and you still see ads, but they are non-personalised. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads, and opt out of ad personalisation there.
  • EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states: a Google-provided consent form appears before the first ad. You can reopen it from that form's "Manage options" to change or withdraw your choices.
  • Remove ads entirely: buy Privio Pro. For a Pro user the ad SDK is never initialised, no ad request is made, and no consent or tracking prompt is shown.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. Its handling is governed by Google's own policies: how Google uses data from apps that use its services and policies.google.com/privacy.

That is the entire list. There is nothing else.

3. Permissions you may grant

Privio asks for the following permissions only at the moment you use the relevant feature:

PermissionWhen askedWhat we do with it
Camera First time you open the document scanner Capture pages in memory, hand them to the on-device scanner (Apple VisionKit on iOS, the on-device document scanner on Android) for cropping and enhancement, write the finished PDF to Privio's private storage. The camera is closed the moment the scan completes.
Photos / Media First time you pick photos for image-to-PDF Read only the photos you select. The system picker (iOS Photos, the Android photo picker) limits us to those specific images. They are processed in memory and the resulting PDF is written to Privio's private storage. The originals stay untouched.

If you decline either permission, the corresponding feature is disabled but the rest of the app works normally.

We do not request location, contacts, microphone, calendar, motion, or any other permission Privio does not need.

4. Storage on your device

Privio writes the following files inside its private app storage (the iOS sandbox, or the Android app-private directory) — readable only by Privio and by you through the system file manager (the iOS Files app, or the Android Files / Downloads UI):

  • Output PDFs you generate — kept until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Tool preferences (paper size, signature font, display name, theme, etc.) via the platform's local key-value store (UserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android).
  • A small local database (Hive) for things like recent files and the optional audit log.
  • A temporary cache of rasterised page images during OCR / redaction, cleared automatically when you leave a tool.

None of these ever leave your device unless you explicitly tap Share inside Privio. The system share sheet (the iOS Share Sheet, or the Android share menu) then sends the file to whatever app you pick (Mail, Files, AirDrop, Drive, etc.). What that destination app does with the file is governed by that app's own privacy policy, not ours.

5. Children's privacy

Privio is a document utility for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and Erek Studio itself holds no user data at all. Privio is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme and is not intended for child-directed use; the advertising described in section 2.3 is therefore not configured for a child audience. If you believe a child has used Privio in a way that needs your attention, contact us at mustafasalimerek@gmail.com.

6. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / similar regimes)

You have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you. The answer is: none. Erek Studio operates no servers and receives no data from the app.
  • Have us delete it. There is nothing on our side to delete. For the advertising data held by Google, use Google's own controls, linked in section 2.3.
  • Withdraw consent for advertising. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and certain US states, reopen the Google consent form from its "Manage options" link. On iOS you can also revoke tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads. Buying Privio Pro stops all ad requests permanently.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe we are mishandling your data.

If you'd still like a written confirmation of "Erek Studio holds no data about you" for your own records, email mustafasalimerek@gmail.com and we will respond in writing within 30 days.

7. Sale and sharing of data

Erek Studio does not sell personal information, and never has. We hold none to sell.

However, under the California CCPA/CPRA — and comparable US state laws — showing personalised ads can count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. The advertising identifiers and ad-interaction data described in section 2.3 are made available to Google for that purpose in the free version of the app.

To opt out: in covered US states the Google consent form appears in-app and lets you decline; on iOS, decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt; on Android, enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" in Settings → Google → Ads. Buying Privio Pro removes ads entirely, which ends the sharing. Your documents are never part of this, under any tier.

8. International transfers

Erek Studio transfers no user data anywhere, because it receives none. The advertising described in section 2.3 is processed by Google on its own global infrastructure, under Google's privacy policy and transfer safeguards. Apple's and Google's separate handling of store purchase data is likewise governed by their own privacy policies.

9. Security

There is no central database, no cloud backup of your PDFs, no server operated by us that could be breached, and no third party that holds your documents. The standard SaaS data-breach risk does not apply to Privio's design.

Your files live in Privio's private app storage (the iOS sandbox, or the Android app-private directory), which the operating system encrypts as long as you have a device passcode or screen lock set. Set one.

If we ever change Privio's data practices, we will rewrite this policy, post the new version under the same URL, and update the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be noted in the in-app What's New of the next update.
