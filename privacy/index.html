PDF Privio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-18
Applies to: PDF Privio 1.1.4 (build 70) and later
Operator: Erek Studio (Mustafa Salim Erek, sole proprietor)
Contact: mustafasalimerek@gmail.com
App: PDF Privio for iOS and Android, com.erekstudio.pdfprivio

Your documents stay on your device

PDF Privio processes PDFs, scans, signatures, form fields, OCR text, summaries and receipt information on your device. We do not send document content or file names to Erek Studio or Firebase. You can explicitly share or export a document to another app; that destination's privacy policy then applies.

PDF Privio has no ads, advertising SDK, account system or third-party crash-reporting SDK. It does include optional Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which parts of the app work well and where people stop before completing a task.

1. Optional usage and purchase statistics

Usage statistics are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Privacy & data → Share usage statistics. Turning them off does not change tool access, prices, purchases or document processing.

When enabled, Firebase receives app lifecycle and session events, onboarding completion, the fixed tool selected, paywall entry point and dismissal, plan category (monthly, yearly or lifetime), purchase outcome and a fixed error category, restore outcome, a scan page-count range, export attempts and successful exports. The only app-defined user property is whether Pro is active. We never set a user ID.

Firebase also processes an app-instance identifier, app/device/OS information, IP-derived approximate location, and automatically measured in-app purchase/subscription events, which may include product ID, product name and price. These statistics are pseudonymous, not a promise of complete anonymity: the app-instance identifier can associate events from the same installation. We do not send your name, email address, display name, document names, file paths, document text, scanned receipt contents, signatures or scanned images.

Advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization consent are denied. iOS IDFA and IDFV collection, Android advertising ID and SSAID collection, automatic screen reporting and iOS advertising-network registration are disabled. No App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown. No document upload is needed to use any tool.

Statistics go to Google/Firebase over encrypted connections. Erek Studio uses the resulting reports only to evaluate app use and the purchase/export funnel. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising. Google processes data under its applicable terms and privacy documentation: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

2. Purchases and platform diagnostics

Apple's App Store or Google Play processes payments and provides purchase/receipt information to the app to unlock Pro locally. We do not receive payment-card details, billing addresses, Apple IDs or Google account identities. With usage statistics enabled, purchase outcomes and Firebase's automatic purchase measurements also reach Analytics as described above.

Apple and Google may separately provide crash, ANR or performance reports under their own OS/store sharing settings. PDF Privio does not include Crashlytics, Firebase Performance or another third-party crash-reporting SDK. Those platform settings are separate from PDF Privio's usage-statistics toggle.

Firebase technical diagnostics

The app sets Firebase's global data-collection default to off, separately from explicitly enabling Analytics when usage statistics are on. This reduces unnecessary Firebase Core heartbeat collection. It is not a universal network-off switch: Firebase Installations may still process installation identifiers and technical SDK/device information, including diagnostic user-agent or heartbeat information, to provide and maintain Firebase services. Such technical diagnostics are separate from optional Analytics and may occur even when usage statistics are off. We do not promise that the usage-statistics switch disables all Google SDK diagnostics.

Google ML Kit diagnostics on Android

Android OCR and the document scanner use Google ML Kit. Pages and recognized text are processed on the device; documents are not uploaded. ML Kit separately sends Google library diagnostic and usage data, including app/device information, installation or device identifiers, feature/API configuration, input/output sizes, latency and error categories. Erek Studio does not receive these ML Kit reports. This collection is separate from Firebase Analytics and is not disabled by the usage-statistics switch. Google's disclosure is at https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and its privacy policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy. On iOS, the app's scanning and OCR call Apple's VisionKit and Vision frameworks instead.

3. Permissions

Camera access is used when you scan pages. The system document/photo picker grants access to the files or photos you select. Scanning and editing happen on the device. PDF Privio does not request location, contacts, microphone or advertising-tracking permissions. Approximate location in Analytics is derived from network addresses, not GPS or a location permission.

4. Local storage

PDF Privio stores generated files, preferences, recent-file references, the audit log, expense ledger and temporary processing files in app storage. These document-related records are not sent to Analytics. They remain until you delete them, the app clears temporary files, or the app is uninstalled, subject to the operating system's backup and storage behavior. A display name entered in Settings is used only for the local greeting.

The usage-statistics preference is saved locally and applied at startup. Turning statistics off stops future Analytics collection; it does not automatically delete statistics already received by Google.

5. Retention, deletion and your rights

Usage statistics are retained under the Google Analytics property's retention settings. You can stop future collection with the in-app toggle. For information, access, correction, objection or deletion requests, contact mustafasalimerek@gmail.com. We do not collect an account identity and may be unable to locate particular pseudonymous event records without additional information. Do not send us documents or sensitive content with a request. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Deleting documents in PDF Privio affects their local copies. Files you shared to another app must be managed with that destination. Store transaction retention and deletion are controlled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

6. International processing and security

Google/Firebase may process statistics outside your country under its applicable data-processing terms. Documents are processed locally; Erek Studio does not maintain a cloud document database. Network statistics use encrypted transport, but no system can promise absolute security. Protect your device with a passcode or screen lock.

7. Children

PDF Privio is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe information about a child requires attention. The usage-statistics toggle is available without creating an account.

8. Policy changes and contact

We will update this policy when data practices change. This version adds optional Firebase Analytics while keeping document processing local, preserves the Android ML Kit diagnostics disclosure, and explains separate Firebase technical diagnostics. Contact mustafasalimerek@gmail.com with privacy questions; we aim to respond within 30 days.
