In particular:

Always review redactions and PII findings by eye before sharing a sensitive document. We are not liable if a leak occurs because the auto-scan missed something.

  • Our redaction tool rebuilds the whole document from page images: it burns black boxes over the words that match your search terms and does not carry the original text layer, metadata, attachments, form fields, links or bookmarks into the new file. It finds only terms that appear in a page’s text layer, so text inside images — including scanned pages that have no text layer — and values typed into form fields are not found; pages without searchable text are listed before you export. See How Privio redacts for the known limitations. Check every redacted PDF before you send it externally: search it for each term and look at every page.
  • Templates we may bundle (court forms, tax forms, immigration forms) are provided as-is for convenience. Forms change. Verify the latest version on the issuing agency's website before filing.
